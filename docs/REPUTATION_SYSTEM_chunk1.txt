# **1. Overview & Design Philosophy**
The Reputation System is the world's memory of the player. It is a four-dimensional record â€” Corps, Tanjiro, Muzan, Demons â€” each measuring how a distinct faction interprets the player's actions. Reputation is entirely separate from Demon Slayer Rank, which is a purely mechanical achievement ladder. A Hashira can be despised by the Corps. A Mizunoto can be feared by every demon in the hierarchy.

The system's central fantasy is the Double Agent: someone who plays all four factions simultaneously by controlling what each one knows. This is achievable but demands active, skilled management of the witness system, concealment tools, and atmospheric conditions. The design respects the Flow state â€” early game is low-stakes and exploratory, pressure builds naturally as the player's reputation grows into ranges that create contradiction.

Core Design Pillars:

Â· Rank and Reputation are separate systems. Never conflate them.

Â· Every dimension supports a full negative range. Being unknown is not the floor.

Â· Actions only reach a faction's ledger if a witness belonging to that faction reports them.

Â· Concealment is always available but always costly in some form.

Â· Demon-path players cannot hide anything from Muzan â€” the blood bond is absolute.

Â· Some actions permanently destroy a faction relationship. These are hard to do accidentally.

Â· Rewards are purely mechanical: stats, access, abilities, story branches. No cosmetics.

# **3. Rank vs. Reputation â€” The Hard Separation**
These are two completely independent systems that happen to share the same player. They never directly modify each other. Rank is a kill-based achievement system. Reputation is a relationship and social record. A player who has killed 1000 demons as a Kinoe is a Hashira regardless of whether the Corps despises them. A player with maximum Corps Rep is not automatically a higher rank.

## **KillRecord Tracking**
Kill counts are tracked per-rank. Kills made as Mizunote do not carry forward to count toward Kanoe requirements. Each rank's counter resets when the player advances. This prevents stockpiling kills before ranking up. The KillRecord stores:

- killsAtCurrentRank â€” count of demon kills since last rank advance
- kizukiSoloKills â€” lifetime count of solo Kizuki kills (used for Hashira alt path)
- totalDemonKills â€” lifetime total (used for taunt content and story flavor)
- killsByCategory â€” Map<DemonCategory, Integer> for mission/story tracking

Rank advancement is automatic â€” no confirmation screen, no trial mission.

When killsAtCurrentRank crosses the threshold, the rank is set immediately and a

notification is sent to the player. The Corps acknowledges advancement passively

through NPC dialogue shifts â€” there is no formal ceremony.

For the Hashira alternate path: a Kizuki solo kill is detected when the player

deals the killing blow to a moon-rank Nemesis demon and no other entity dealt

damage to that demon during the current BattleSession. This uses the existing

BattleSession.interferers check from the Nemesis system.

# **5. The Four Reputation Dimensions**
## **5.1  Corps Reputation**
Measures how DSHQ perceives the player as an agent, a soldier, and a person. High Corps Rep opens mission pools, changes how Hashira and NPCs speak to the player, and reduces the baseline rate at which Suspicion accumulates. Negative Corps Rep means the player is disgraced â€” Hashira are dispatched to bring them in or cut them down.

Corps Rep is also the only dimension the player can see a named tier for on demand â€” by speaking to Corps-aligned NPCs who will tell the player where they stand. The hidden Suspicion sub-meter (0â€“500) runs in parallel and is never shown directly.

## **5.2  Tanjiro Affinity**
Measures Tanjiro's personal trust in the player. It does not mirror Corps Rep. Tanjiro fights beside anyone in danger regardless of affinity tier â€” his character demands it. What affinity changes is how far he is willing to deviate from the anime's main plot on the player's behalf: sharing restricted information, hiding evidence, altering his own plans around the player's actions, and unlocking mission branches that do not exist in the base story.

Negative affinity means Tanjiro actively avoids the player. At deep negative he treats the player as an obstacle or threat and will engage them in combat if they interfere with his mission. He never permanently turns hostile unless the player kills a crew member â€” but at low affinity his combat assistance vanishes and his branching cooperation closes entirely.

## **5.3  Muzan's Favor**
Measures how Muzan regards the player. Positive favor does not mean Muzan calls off demon hunts â€” the Nemesis system's hunters are acting on their own motivations (territory, rivalry, ambition). Muzan actively wants demons fighting each other and resists allowing any demon to grow too powerful by making alliances. What positive Muzan favor does is: reduce the probability he personally intervenes, unlock demon-path content, and occasionally cause a Kizuki to pause before acting against the player.

Negative Muzan favor is where his power becomes direct. At sufficiently negative tiers, Muzan issues personal directives â€” he dispatches specific Kizuki to hunt the player, functioning identically to how he dispatched hunters against Tanjiro in the source material. The more negative the favor, the higher-ranked the Kizuki he sends. This is separate from and on top of the standard Nemesis hunter spawning.

Demon-path players (those who have drunk Muzan's blood) cannot conceal anything from Muzan. Every action they take is transmitted through the blood bond. There is no witness mechanic available for this channel.

## **5.4  Demon Notoriety**
Measures how the demon world perceives the player as a combatant. Positive notoriety means the player is known, feared, and respected â€” cowardly demons flee, ambitious ones seek the challenge, and demon taunts reference the player's deeds by name. Negative notoriety means the player is known as weak â€” a soft target. Cowardly demons who would normally flee now attack, while powerful duelist-personality demons consider the player beneath their attention entirely.

Demon Notoriety feeds directly into the Nemesis System's spawn chance and behavior override formulas. It is the most mechanically consequential dimension in combat.

# **7. Faction Hard Lines â€” Permanent Blacklists**
Hard line violations are stored as HardlineFlag enum values in the player's record. They are never cleared under any circumstance â€” no redemption arc, no quest, no command exists to reverse them in normal gameplay. Because they are permanent, they must be extremely difficult to trigger accidentally. Each is gated behind a multi-step confirmation or requires a deliberate, sustained action.
